Fosroc / Sika applicator — application guide for 91SkylineWorks

Why: SCS (Beharbari) ranks on Maps partly because they are a listed Fosroc/Sika/Hilti applicator. Match the badge, keep our diagnosis-first story.

Priority: Fosroc first (waterproofing + repair overlap), Sika second.

Before you apply
----------------
- 20+ GBP photos uploaded (site work, terrace, column repair, team)
- 3+ case studies live on https://91skylineworks.com/portfolio
- Written diagnosis pack offered on every inspection (site already live)
- Company docs: GST, MSME, company registration

Fosroc India — applicator / applicator contractor
-------------------------------------------------
1. Contact Fosroc India NE regional office or via https://www.fosroc.com/india/
2. Ask for: "Authorised applicator" or "Applicator contractor" for waterproofing and concrete repair in Guwahati
3. Provide: company profile, 3 project references (Beltola terrace, Dispur column, GS Road joints), photos, insurance if available
4. Once approved: add badge to GBP, About page, and proposal PDFs

Sika India — authorised applicator
----------------------------------
1. Contact via https://ind.sika.com/en/applicator.html or regional sales
2. AKJ Engineers (akjengineers.in) is existing Sika distributor in Guwahati — ask if subcontractor applicator partnership is possible
3. Target: listed on Sika applicator finder for Assam
